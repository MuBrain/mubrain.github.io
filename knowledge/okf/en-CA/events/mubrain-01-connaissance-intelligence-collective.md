# MuBrain #1 — From Knowledge to Collective Intelligence

## Intent

Relaunch MuBrain with a foundational meetup that starts not with tools, but with the core problem: how can an organization turn its information assets into a collective capacity to understand, learn, decide, and act?

## Working questions

- What distinguishes information, knowledge, and collective intelligence?
- Where does an organization's intelligence actually reside?
- What roles do people, systems, and AI agents play in that intelligence?
- What traces truly allow an organization to learn?

## Format

MuBrain 5@7, from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., 100% in person in Montreal. Short introduction, framing of the issue, structured discussion, synthesis of disagreements, and research directions.

## Location

AFI Expertise  
500 Saint-Jacques Street, Suite 500  
Montreal, QC H2Y 1S1

## LinkedIn description

MuBrain is back, and we want to begin with the most fundamental question.

An organization can accumulate thousands of documents, years of conversations, and a tremendous amount of expertise without necessarily becoming more intelligent.

Having knowledge is not the same as having collective intelligence.

For this first meetup in the new MuBrain cycle, we will work on that transition: how can an organization turn its information assets, the experience of its members, its decisions, and now its AI agents into a collective capacity to understand, learn, decide, and act?

We will begin with a few deceptively simple questions:

• What distinguishes a knowledge base from collective intelligence?  
• Where does an organization's intelligence actually reside?  
• What role can generative AI and agents play?  
• What traces should we preserve so the organization can learn?

This will not be a conventional conference.

MuBrain is a think tank: a few propositions will provide the starting point, but most of the meetup will be devoted to discussion, challenge, and collective construction.

This first meetup will also help establish the foundations for MuBrain's next research activities and publications.
