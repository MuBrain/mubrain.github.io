# MuBrain #4 — Governing Hybrid Intelligence: Humans, the Collective, and Agents

## Intent

Move MuBrain's work from a reflection on organizational intelligence toward a reflection on its governance.

After exploring the transformation of knowledge into collective intelligence, the boundaries between individual and organizational intelligence, and then the architecture of the Enterprise Brain, this fourth meetup addresses authority, accountability, and the rules required when people and agents contribute together to action.

## Working questions

- Who holds decision-making authority in a hybrid system?
- Which decisions should remain explicitly human?
- How should accountability be assigned when an agent contributes to a decision or action?
- How can provenance, context, and assumptions remain verifiable?
- How should agent autonomy be governed?
- How can an organization preserve its capacity to learn without losing control?

## Format

MuBrain 5@7, from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., 100% in person in Montreal. Date and venue to be confirmed.

## LinkedIn description

When an organization begins to make people, collectives, and AI agents work together, the question is no longer only technological.

It becomes a governance question.

Who can decide what?

Who is accountable when an agent contributes to a decision?

Which decisions should remain explicitly human?

How can we make visible the sources, assumptions, and reasoning that led to an action?

And how far should an organization allow its agents to act autonomously?

This fourth MuBrain meetup will focus on the governance of hybrid intelligence.

After working on the transition from knowledge to collective intelligence, the boundaries between individual and organizational intelligence, and then the architecture of the Enterprise Brain, we now want to examine the rules that allow the whole system to function without becoming a new black box.

We will confront questions of authority, accountability, provenance, transparency, autonomy, and human control.

As with the other MuBrain meetups, this will not be a top-down conference.

A few propositions will provide the starting point, and then we will work collectively on disagreements, limits, and the principles that could form a MuBrain governance framework for hybrid organizational intelligence.
