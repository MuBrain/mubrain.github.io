# Open Knowledge Format

The Open Knowledge Format, or OKF, is a way of structuring knowledge as Markdown files enriched with YAML frontmatter.

In MuBrain, OKF serves as an inspirational model for organizing knowledge in a simple, durable way that AI agents can use.

## Why this matters for MuBrain

MuBrain should not be just a website. It should become a structured knowledge space.

This means that content must be:

- readable by humans;
- version-controlled in GitHub;
- understandable by AI agents;
- easy to translate;
- separated into public content and reference knowledge.
