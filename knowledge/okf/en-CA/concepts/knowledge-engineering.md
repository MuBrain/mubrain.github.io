# Knowledge Engineering for Generative Systems

Knowledge Engineering is the discipline of designing, structuring,
maintaining, and governing knowledge to optimize its simultaneous use
by humans and artificial intelligence.

## Hypothesis

Given models of equivalent quality, explicit knowledge structuring
has a measurable influence on the quality, reproducibility, and stability
of responses produced by generative artificial intelligence.

## Desired properties

Knowledge designed for generative systems should be:

- versionable;
- modular and composable;
- traceable;
- reusable;
- portable across different assistants;
- governed as an information asset.
