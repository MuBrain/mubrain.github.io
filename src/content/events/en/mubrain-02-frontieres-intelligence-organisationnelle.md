Part of an organization's intelligence lives in its systems, processes, and documents. Another part lives in people: their experience, relationships, intuition, and understanding of context.

And tomorrow, part of that intelligence will also be carried by agents.

This MuBrain meetup will explore a deliberately difficult question: **where are the boundaries between the intelligence of the organization and that of the individuals who make it up?**

We will look in particular at the onboarding and offboarding of intelligence: how can a person genuinely enrich the collective without the organization pretending to absorb what belongs to that person? And how can a departure avoid taking with it a critical part of the organization's memory and capacity for action?

The meetup will take place as a **5@7, 100% in person**, with a short introduction followed by structured discussion.

### Proposed questions

- What belongs to the collective, and what remains individual?
- Can some forms of intellectual contribution be governed contractually without confusing them with ownership of a person?
- How can the context of a decision be captured without capturing the individual?
- How do AI agents change these boundaries?

The objective is not to reach a definitive answer, but to build a sufficiently robust framework for further MuBrain research.
