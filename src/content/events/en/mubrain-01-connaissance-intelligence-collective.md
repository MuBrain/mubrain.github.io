An organization can accumulate thousands of documents, years of conversations, and a tremendous amount of expertise without necessarily becoming more intelligent.

This first MuBrain meetup starts from a fundamental distinction: **having knowledge is not the same as having collective intelligence**.

Together, we will explore what enables an organization to turn its information assets, the experience of its members, its decisions, and now its AI agents into a collective capacity to understand, learn, decide, and act.

The meetup will take the form of a **MuBrain 5@7**, 100% in person. A few propositions will provide the starting point, but most of the time will be devoted to discussion, challenge, and collective construction.

### Proposed questions

- What distinguishes a knowledge base from collective intelligence?
- Where does an organization's intelligence actually reside?
- What role can generative AI and agents play without reducing the Enterprise Brain to another technology platform?
- What traces should we preserve so the organization can learn?

This meetup opens the new MuBrain research cycle.
